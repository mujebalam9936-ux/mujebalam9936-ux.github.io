About PracticalShelf

PracticalShelf shares practical ideas and printable math and literacy resources for children aged 4 to 9: color by number, count and flip books, hundreds chart mystery pictures, morning work, sight words and phonics. You may also know us from our PracticalShelf Facebook page and YouTube channel.

What we care about. Every page has to teach something real and be something a child actually wants to finish. A color by number page only works if the math is right on every space, so each resource goes through automated checks (every answer, every color, every printed number) and a review before it is published. Answer keys come with everything.

Made for busy adults. Most of our resources are no prep: print in black and white and hand out crayons. Many come in two to four levels, so one picture works for the whole class or for siblings of different ages at home.

Where to get them. Our resources are sold in our UESArt store on Teachers Pay Teachers, where you can look through the full preview before you buy. If you want to try us first, start with a free sampler.

This blog shares practical ideas for using printables well: how to set up a center, how to differentiate one worksheet for a whole class, and what to do in the busy weeks of the school year.

Questions or a request for a resource? Ask on any of our product pages on TPT; we read and answer every question.
